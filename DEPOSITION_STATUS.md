availability statements can be checked against reality rather than intent.

[`README.md`](README.md) and [`results/README.md`](results/README.md).

## Deliberate, permanent non-deposition

These are stated in the paper's availability statements themselves.

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| CAMI II sequence data | Third-party. Openly available at <https://frl.publisso.de/data/frl:6425521/>. Only identifiers, derived truth tables, bin definitions and the leakage audit are deposited. |
| ATCC Genome Portal genomes | The ATCC Data Use Agreement is incompatible with unrestricted redistribution. These genomes were **not used** in the study. |
| Meslier mock-community and ZymoBIOMICS assemblies | Third-party, obtainable from their providers (ENA PRJEB52977, PRJEB29504). Accessions, derived truth and predictions are deposited. |
| SPIRE v1, GTDB r220, UHGG v2.0.2 genomes | Third-party catalogues. The genome identifiers analysed are deposited. |
| GTDB / NCBI reference assemblies (345 GB) | Identified by accession in `splits/`, `curation/` and `provenance/`; downloadable from NCBI. |
| Raw tool output trees (~25 GB) | Intermediates. Itemised with sizes and reasons in [`results/README.md`](results/README.md); the parsed per-genome predictions and consolidated tables are deposited. |
| The three taxonomic-holdout ONNX models (`magicc_holdout_{phylum,family,genus}.onnx`, 162–170 MB each, ~494 MB) | Validation artefacts, not released models, and promised by neither availability statement. Each is regenerated by the deposited training scripts from the deposited split files. Their **evaluation outputs are deposited in full**; only the weights are not. For the genus model the SHA256 (`e9647b53…`) and the ONNX-versus-PyTorch equivalence check are deposited so a regenerated copy is checkable. |

## Known limitations of what *is* deposited

| Item | Limitation |
|---|---|
| Per-sample metadata for **sets A, B and E** | These three sets predate the full generation record. `metadata.tsv` carries ground truth, dominant accession and phylum, sample type, contig count, total length and the design target — but **not** the per-sample contaminant accessions or fragmentation parameters, and no `seed` column. What is released instead is the exact rule that regenerates them: `provenance/seed_provenance.tsv` gives the generator, base seed and per-sample RNG construction. Sets C-clean, D-clean, F, G and H carry the complete 43–48-column `generation_metadata.tsv`. |
| Reports under `results/` | Written inside the analysis workspace; they refer to paths of the form `results/revision/<x>`, which here are `results/<x>`. The three exceptions are mapped in [`results/README.md`](results/README.md). |
| `scripts/` numbering | Renumbered for this deposition to remove collisions; the working tree was not renumbered. [`scripts/SCRIPT_MAPPING.tsv`](scripts/SCRIPT_MAPPING.tsv) is the full mapping, 263 rows. Cross-references inside `scripts/` were repointed at the deposited names so the set runs as it stands; reports under `results/` keep the working-tree names, because they record what was run. |
| Two dangling references inside `scripts/` | Both predate this deposition and are left as they are, because both are true statements about the workspace: `081_kraken2_db_capacity_comparison.py` names `082_build_gtdb_kraken2_db.sh` where the file is `.py` (that GTDB Kraken2 rebuild was stood down and never completed), and `199_ws38_run_competitors_cami2.sh` names `200_ws38_cami2_select_competitor_cohort.py`, which was later merged into `200_ws38_cami2_cohort_and_collect.py`. |
| Script snapshot | `scripts/` is a snapshot taken 2026-08-27. Three workstreams (leave-genus-out, catalogue-scale SPIRE, the v0.3.3 timing sub-campaign) were still adding scripts on that day; everything that existed at the snapshot is here. |
